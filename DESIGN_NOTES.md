# MingleKE design notes

Direction (2026-09-23): **Nairobi lookbook / street zine**. The product is portraits,
so chrome stays quiet and the photo plate does the talking.

Tokens: ink `#14110E` · bone `#EDE6DC` · hibiscus `#D62839` · clay `#8A7355` · moss `#2F5D50`.
Display is condensed uppercase (nameplates, masthead). Meta is monospace (distance, age).
Radius max 6px. No glass, no gradient text, no blur orbs, no pill nav.

What looked vibecoded before and should not come back:
- glass-panel + purple night + rose Tinder pink
- gradient-text, floating blur circles, heart confetti
- `rounded-[24px]` / `[28px]` / `[32px]` roulette
- copy like “meaningful social discovery starts now”

Signature: bone nameplate strip on a full-bleed photo. That is the one loud move.
